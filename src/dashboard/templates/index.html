⚡ 1. PCDU (Power Conditioning & Distribution) CORE HUB

Physical Role: Central electrical distribution routing power from solar arrays and batteries to satellite payloads. Manages primary Bus-A (28.4V regulated) and redundant Bus-B (hot standby).

• Simulated Sub-Components: Remote Power Controllers (RPC-1 and RPC-2), sub-20ms solid-state latching relays.
• Physical Limit: Bus collapse threshold $V < 2.90\,\text{V}$; peak continuous current $5.0\,\text{A}$.
• Actuation: Trips non-essential science loads ($-35\%$ or $-75\%$) under commanded load shedding.
☀️ 2. BCR (Battery Charge Regulator) SOLAR / REG

Physical Role: Regulates solar array current entering the battery pack and dissipates excess power through Sequential Switching Shunt Regulators (S3R).

• Simulated Sub-Components: Constant-Current (2.4A CC) fast charger, Trickle charge mode ($C/20 = 0.5\text{A}$), shunt limiters.
• Physical Limit: Max solar array output $1361\,\text{W/m}^2$; max charge rate $1\text{C} = 4.8\,\text{A}$.
• Actuation: Automatically tapers charge to Trickle $C/20$ when cell temperature exceeds $45^\circ\text{C}$.
🔋 3. Secondary Li-ion Battery Pack ENERGY STORE

Physical Role: 8S4P Li-ion cell bank providing continuous satellite bus power during the 30-minute orbital eclipse.

• Simulated Chemistry: Dynamic internal impedance proxy $R_{\text{int}}(t) = \frac{|\Delta V|}{|\Delta I| + \epsilon}$, Coulombic efficiency (99.2%), Joule heat generation ($I^2 R_{\text{int}}$).

• Degradation: SEI layer growth doubles $R_{\text{int}}$ over operational lifespan.
🛰️ 4. Spacecraft Radiator & ADCS Thermal TCS & ATTITUDE
